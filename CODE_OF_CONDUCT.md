# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate regardless of background, identity, or experience. Be
respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the project and its users.

## Expected behavior

- Give constructive feedback about ideas and code, and respect differing perspectives.
- Use clear, considerate language and accept corrections when you make a mistake.
- Respect privacy. Use synthetic examples and remove credentials and account data from reports.
- Help keep issues and pull requests useful and accessible to other contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. These rules
apply in repository issues, pull requests, comments, and other spaces maintained by this project.

## Reporting and moderation

For abusive content, use GitHub's reporting controls on the relevant content. To request
maintainer help with another conduct concern, open a brief issue asking for a private contact
channel without naming affected people or sharing sensitive details publicly. Security
vulnerabilities follow the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close disruptive conversations, or restrict participation
when needed to protect the community. They should consider the context, explain moderation
decisions when appropriate, and respect the privacy of people involved.
